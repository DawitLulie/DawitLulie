## 🚀 About Me

<p align="center">
  I'm <b>Dawit Lulie</b>, a <b>Software Engineering student at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU)</b>, a <b>Full-Stack Developer</b>, and a <b>Competitive Programmer</b> passionate about solving challenging problems and engineering impactful technology.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Beyond writing code, I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood — from designing efficient algorithms and scalable backend architectures to exploring Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Transformer-based models.
</p>

<p align="center">
  My journey involves continuous learning, engineering, and collaboration through communities and organizations including <b>A2SV</b>, <b>iCog Labs</b>, <b>Gebeta Maps</b>, and <b>INSA</b>.
</p>

<p align="center">
  I enjoy working across the entire development lifecycle — from requirements engineering and system design to building responsive web applications, cross-platform mobile apps, intelligent AI-powered systems, and production-ready backend services.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <b>My philosophy:</b> Don't just write code. Understand the problem, design the solution, optimize the execution, and build something that matters.
</p>
